Help & Civic Guidelines

Contact Municipal Helpline

For urgent interventions or life-threatening situations, please dial the 24/7 hotline: 1800-WARD-HELP.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does resolution take? Standard resolution SLA is 7 working days depending on the priority category.
  • Can I remain anonymous? System records are mandatory for accountability, but public visibility is restricted to Ward Members.
  • What size images can I upload? Optimal images are JPEG/PNG formats below 10MB in file size.
